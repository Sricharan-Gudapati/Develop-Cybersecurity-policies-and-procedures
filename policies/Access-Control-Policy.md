# Access Control Policy

## 1. Overview
This Access Control Policy defines the requirements for granting, modifying, and revoking access to organizational systems, data, and network resources. The goal is to ensure the principle of least privilege and protect sensitive assets from unauthorized disclosure or modification.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, third-party vendors, and any authorized personnel who access company infrastructure, applications, or data repositories.

## 3. Principles & Guidelines

### 3.1 Principle of Least Privilege
* Users shall be granted the minimum level of access necessary to perform their specific job functions.
* Administrative privileges must be restricted to authorized IT and security personnel only.

### 3.2 Authentication & Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
* All users must authenticate using strong passwords that meet complexity requirements (minimum 12 characters, mixing uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols).
* **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)** is strictly mandatory for accessing all corporate networks, cloud environments, email systems, and production servers.

### 3.3 Access Reviews
* User access rights must be formally reviewed on a **quarterly basis** by department managers.
* Any unnecessary, orphaned, or elevated accounts that are no longer required must be disabled immediately.

## 4. Onboarding and Offboarding
* **Onboarding:** Access requests must be submitted by hiring managers and approved by IT Security before account provisioning.
* **Offboarding:** Access for terminated or departing employees must be revoked within **1 hour** of the effective termination time to prevent insider risk.

## 5. Enforcement and Compliance
Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or revocation of contract agreements.
